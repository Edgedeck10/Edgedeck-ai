# EdgeDeck — The Research Playbook
Mobile-first sports analytics on public GitHub Pages and standard GitHub-hosted Actions. No paid services, API keys, or automated betting.

## Sport workspaces
Independent NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB, college basketball/football, WNBA, EPL, MLS, golf, ATP/WTA and UFC tabs. Each has a sport-specific market rail, game cards, featured players where public roster images exist, a daily best single, 2–10-leg daily ticket cards and its own record. Unavailable sports/markets are explicitly import-only; a tab is not a claim of automated coverage. Navy/Irish-green field, white cards and polished gold wraps, custom shamrock crest and accents, responsive navigation.

## Official daily selection record
`daily_history.json` freezes the first qualified selection for each Eastern-time day/sport/ticket size before start. One best single and separate 2 through 10-leg records. Selection, original quote, probability and timestamp never change; outcomes append after final results. A losing leg loses the ticket; pending legs keep it pending unless already lost; pushes remove legs for hypothetical payout, all-push tickets refund. Win rate is wins/(wins+losses); pending and push counts shown separately. One-unit hypothetical ROI and 95% Wilson intervals accompany the record. CSV export and original ticket inspection are available.

Eligible automatic picks require an actual observed single-market quote between -200 and +200 (valid American odds), at least 10 samples, quote collected within 35 minutes, 10+ minutes before start, 3–15 probability-point estimated edge, 5% estimated ROI and positive risk-buffered edge. Questionable availability, major role change, outlier sensitivity and unconfirmed goalies are excluded. Rank by buffered expected return; one selection per game, no repeated player. Tickets freeze when enough selections qualify; no filler or retrospective selections. This is a heuristic ranking, not a proven optimizer.

Parlay displayed prices multiply quoted single-leg decimal prices: **not a verified sportsbook parlay quote**. Joint hit estimates assume independent legs; shared model errors, book repricing and dependencies remain unmodeled. Each size has a separate record but overlapping tickets are correlated. Goals of 85–90% singles and 65–80% parlays are ambitions, not measured achievements or a professional baseline. Five independent 90% legs yield 59.05%; no success rate is promised.

## Projections and history
Player production rate per minute/opportunity × expected exposure × shrunk opponent positional allowance vs league positional baseline. Position buckets are not individual defender assignments. Football uses attempts/targets, hockey TOI/shots faced, missing exposures flagged. Venue effects require split evidence; teammate-absence adjustments require actual DNP split evidence; rest adjustments are heuristic. Confirmed rotations, defensive assignments, rebound chances, potential assists, schedule strength and full correlation models remain gaps.

Use up to 50 available current-season comparable games from cached schedules (60-day retrieval window), current team when at least five games exist, otherwise current-season data. Outliers stay in the sample; MAD checks and exposure shifts add flags/uncertainty. Early seasons may have fewer games. Charts show actual last 10/15/25 with date/opponent, threshold line and H2H filter; missing games are never fabricated. The 80–90% research screen requires 10+ samples and excludes role/outlier/availability flags; its estimates are not validated hit rates.

Provisional normal/empirical-residual probability ensemble, uncertainty inflation and heuristic risk buffer. Rare events need future count models. Gradual prospective calibration uses only settled forecasts from the same model version and groups correlated alternate lines by event/entity/market/side. Old forecasts are never rewritten. Sport-level calibration is separate from daily selection performance. Legacy research shortlist retained separately from the new official daily ledger.

Free ESPN game/roster/box-score/injury endpoints provide observed DraftKings game-line snapshots where available. No automatic player-prop sportsbook quotes. Suggested lines and illustrative historical-baseline prices (hypothetical 4% overround) are labeled as research estimates, never observed offers. Exact-line editor accepts real prices. Imported personal lines remain on-device and are separate from official performance.

Free Open-Meteo city-level hourly weather is shown when venue resolves without ambiguity. Wind/rain modest heuristic passing/receiving adjustments apply only to confirmed outdoor NFL/CFB venues. Unknown roofs, missing/ambiguous locations and feed errors stay neutral, with gaps labeled. This is not live stadium weather.

## Refresh, persistence and deployment
`.github/workflows/refresh.yml`: every 15 minutes (:07/:22/:37/:52 UTC), source pushes and manual dispatch. GitHub cron may run late. Page polls the latest published feed while visible; Check latest reloads published data, not backend collection. Schedule/roster caches daily; up to 180 boxscores per run, NBA first, remaining queue disclosed. Public free runners and hosting only.

Full immutable line history is preserved in deterministic monthly gzip JSON archives, indexed by `history_manifest.json`. All archive files are published for download; `history.json` holds the latest 5,000 forecasts for fast phone inspection. The entire daily best-pick ledger is published. Shared per-player model details are normalized in a gzip-compressed latest.json.gz and hydrated by the browser to reduce transfer size; current Safari/Chrome support is required. feed_status.json provides a small inspectable build summary. No destructive retention of historical forecasts.

## Validation
`python -m unittest discover -p 'test_*.py'` and `node test_ui.cjs`; syntax checks on app.js, analysis-ui.js and sports-ui.js. Tests cover settlement, calibration, quote sensitivity, immutable daily picks, all ticket sizes, push/pending/loss handling, duplicate-game protection, archives, role/weather checks and charts. Serve with `python -m http.server` for UI checks.

## Permissions
Workflow needs contents:write, pages:write and id-token:write, with GitHub Pages set to Actions. No secrets required. Repository and public forecasts are public. Do not import credentials. No account balances, deposits, sportsbook accounts or wagers are controlled by the app.

## Confidence-first daily policy (v4.1 UI)
Daily v2 ranks positive-value observed quotes by heuristic risk-buffered hit probability, then buffered edge and sample. Singles keep the +/-200 price band and require >=60% model estimate. This is not an 85% success claim. Parlays use >=80% legs at observed -1500 through +200 prices, >=65% independent joint estimate and >=50% buffered product. Quote freshness, 10+ samples, 3–15 pp edge and >=5% expected ROI remain gates. Missing, ambiguous or unresolved player inputs exclude selection. No filler picks. Independence is not verified; the buffered product is not a statistical lower bound. Mathematical joint bounds are shown separately.

Evidence is frozen with each new leg: numerical thesis, scoring or exposure/position inputs, historical threshold chart, quote/break-even/edge, injury and weather feed context, explicit failure cases, source and calibration sample. Team previous-game covers are descriptive against different opponents, not a forecast backtest. Existing old-policy records remain intact and continue grading; new policy is separately identified and daily overview shows current-policy picks. Lifetime scorecards include every policy; a policy update does not reset losses. Every sport has a daily status, including unavailable automatic data, no returned slate and no qualified pick.

The daily alternate-line watchlist is a separate unpriced research surface, up to three stable players from different events per sport. Requires 80–90% model estimate and 80%+ descriptive historical clearing at the exact threshold, ten samples and no unresolved role/availability flags. Rank by the smaller of buffered model probability and historical rate. This deliberately selects on historical performance; it is not an out-of-sample test and no quoted payoff or verified edge is implied. It never enters the official quoted daily ledger. Two/three-leg structures are preferred for research over long tickets; rollover ladder staking is not automated.

Public strategy references: https://linemaker.ai/quickstart and https://linemaker.ai/research-disclaimer describe alternate favorites, historical season data, observed quote break-even and positive estimated edge; this is inspiration, not access to their proprietary method. Season clearing in new evidence is separated where archived data exists, otherwise explicitly unavailable. Minutes-qualified streaks are not unconditional probabilities. No subscription, trial or paid feed was activated.

## Week-ahead selections and live reviews (v4.2)
All supported scoreboards are queried through seven days ahead. All returned pregame games in the capped collection window receive fresh summary availability checks each run. Schedule history retrieval extends to 120 days; player models still require comparable season windows. Each sport's board now shows the coming week, with exact event start times.

Daily v3 assembles quoted positive-value comparisons with 60%+ legs, 10 samples, fresh quotes and the original edge/ROI gates. Below-65% joint estimates are visible with an explicit below-target label; they are not promoted as high-confidence recommendations. The former hard 65% visibility gate prevented all parlay cards and has been removed. New-policy tickets remain distinct from prior records.

The unquoted alternate-line watchlist is now assembled automatically into 2–10-leg research tickets where enough different events and players qualify. Original thresholds and probabilities freeze once per day/size/sport; outcomes grade automatically in research_ticket_history.json. Research win/loss counts are separate from quoted performance. Missing sportsbook quotes mean no payout/ROI/verified edge claim. These research records describe hypothetical thresholds, not wagers or confirmed offers.

Public ESPN league-news feeds refresh alongside injuries and quoted prices. Source-linked, published-time headlines are matched by team IDs or full names. Keyword alerts flag potential roster/injury/minutes/rest developments for review; text is not converted to an invented quantitative model impact. Collection failures retain older articles with a failure status. Every frozen ticket gets a separate current review for line/price changes, injury reports, news and event state; original forecast values remain unchanged. News is incomplete, not an exhaustive real-time monitoring service. Golf/tennis/UFC betting projections still lack an automatic verified odds/history source; news availability does not imply betting-model coverage.

Individual-sport PGA/ATP/WTA/UFC calendars and competition fixtures are queried for the coming week and source-linked when returned. Fixture coverage is explicitly separate from betting models/quotes. No odds, edges, probabilities or picks are invented from a schedule alone.

Football uses a sport-specific eight-game minimum for team-market comparisons and unquoted research. Samples below ten remain explicitly marked limited; team forecasts use an eight-percentage-point heuristic risk buffer before value screening. Other quoted player models retain ten games. This is a provisional research choice, not evidence of an 85% success rate.


## 4.3 · Compact picks and FanDuel verification
The main header is compact; coverage, news, selection methodology and counts are in a bottom disclosure. All picks combines every sport, with verified selections separated from unquoted research. Original bookmaker tickets remain in the permanent record.

Official daily selection policy now requires a fresh FanDuel Indiana quote, explicit verification and quote provenance, in addition to the statistical/value checks. The current free ESPN feed does **not** supply that verification, player-prop selection IDs or a combined FanDuel slip link. Consequently the app correctly shows no confirmed FanDuel recommendation. No service, subscription, trial or payment has been activated. This is a data-access blocker, not a GitHub permission issue. The generic FanDuel button is explicitly manual search; it transfers no selections. A single-selection deep link is enabled only when an actual verified source supplies its FanDuel market/selection URL. Combined-slip transfer remains unavailable without an actual verified combined link.

Every active generated prop threshold with a perfect recorded comparable sample (minimum 10 games, next seven days, excluding out/doubtful players) is listed by sport, with sample size, model probability, fair odds and flags. Generated thresholds are not asserted to be available at FanDuel. The list uses the same untrimmed current-team/current-season model sample; unavailable earlier seasons remain missing. 100% past clearing is never a 100% forecast. Stable-role candidates are ranked by buffered model probability, then sample size. The 5-, 7-, and 9-leg research options select only the top independent events/players, freeze once daily, and grade in `data/perfect_ticket_history.json`. No filler tickets if too few events qualify, no invented parlay price, no ROI for unquoted thresholds. One event/player reduces obvious correlation but does not validate independence.


## 4.4 · Estimated-line mode authorized
Model-estimated alternate lines are allowed for best singles and every parlay, independent of FanDuel connectivity. All cards show exact threshold, model probability and model fair American odds; illustrative market-price estimates are labeled separately when captured. Joint fair prices assume independent legs and are not offered parlay payouts. No paid feed is enabled. The verified FanDuel gate remains for actual FanDuel quotes and deep links only.

Estimated best singles use unquoted player targets, at least 10 comparable games (8 for football, labeled limited), stable opportunity/availability checks, at least 50% full-sample historical clearing, and 55%–66.67% model estimates to retain model fair prices near even money through -200. Rank by the smaller of buffered model probability and historical clearing, then sample. This ranks confidence within a price band, not verified expected value. Exact line, original probability and evidence freeze once per day/sport in `data/estimated_best_history.json`; future outcomes are graded automatically, separately from quoted ROI. Existing 2–10-leg and perfect-history 5/7/9 research records remain unchanged. Sports with no usable data show an evidence gap instead of invented picks.


## 4.5 · Alternate overs only, every sport tab
Unquoted player models, estimated best singles, alternate-line parlays and perfect-history lists now recommend overs only. Alternate unders are excluded from the board, automated/manual parlay pools and active ticket surfaces, including stale cached reports. Original observed main game-market under quotes remain available as distinct markets. This implements the owner’s preference; it does not assert universal sportsbook market availability.

New over-only policy versions freeze replacement selections once per day/sport/size. All previous tickets, including unders and any losses, remain immutable and continue grading; summary records include the earlier policies so losses are not erased. Each sport uses the same dedicated best-bet, 2–10-leg parlay, perfect-history and record sections as NBA, with sport-specific evidence gaps and upcoming event lists. NBA/NFL/NHL/CFB have current usable projections; other sports require sufficient scheduled games/history, and golf/tennis/UFC still lack connected projection models. No invented recommendations or paid services.


## 4.7 · Transparent modeling and price theory
Public Rithmm methodology inspired clearer separation of player projections, observed trends, price edge, contextual evidence and forward validation. This is EdgeDeck’s own arithmetic, not Rithmm model access. Each recommended ticket now has a model audit: projection vs threshold, rate/opportunities when frozen, sample and calibration-group counts, ±5pp per-leg sensitivity scenarios, and an exact single/full-parlay price checker. Positive value requires the entered offer to exceed buffered break-even probability; estimated market prices do not qualify as verified edge. Expected ROI is hypothetical and separate from measured results. Inputs do not overwrite any frozen forecast. Alternate overs only remains enforced. No subscription/trial, proprietary inputs, backtest record or new trained model is claimed.
