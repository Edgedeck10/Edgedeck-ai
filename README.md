# EdgeDeck — The Research Playbook
Mobile-first sports analytics on public GitHub Pages and standard GitHub-hosted Actions. No paid services, API keys, or automated betting.

## Sport workspaces
Independent NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB, college basketball/football, WNBA, EPL, MLS, golf, ATP/WTA and UFC tabs. Each has a sport-specific market rail, game cards, featured players where public roster images exist, a daily best single, 2–10-leg daily ticket cards and its own record. Unavailable sports/markets are explicitly import-only; a tab is not a claim of automated coverage. Navy/Irish-green field, white cards and polished gold wraps, custom shamrock crest and accents, responsive navigation.

## Official daily selection record
`daily_history.json` freezes the first qualified selection for each Eastern-time day/sport/ticket size before start. One best single and separate 2 through 10-leg records. Selection, original quote, probability and timestamp never change; outcomes append after final results. A losing leg loses the ticket; pending legs keep it pending unless already lost; pushes remove legs for hypothetical payout, all-push tickets refund. Win rate is wins/(wins+losses); pending and push counts shown separately. One-unit hypothetical ROI and 95% Wilson intervals accompany the record. CSV export and original ticket inspection are available.

Eligible automatic picks require an actual observed single-market quote between -200 and +200 (valid American odds), at least 10 samples, quote collected within 35 minutes, 10+ minutes before start, 3–15 probability-point estimated edge, 5% estimated ROI and positive risk-buffered edge. Questionable availability, major role change, outlier sensitivity and unconfirmed goalies are excluded. Rank by buffered expected return; one selection per game, no repeated player. Tickets freeze when enough selections qualify; no filler or retrospective selections. This is a heuristic ranking, not a proven optimizer.

Parlay displayed prices multiply quoted single-leg decimal prices: **not a verified sportsbook parlay quote**. Joint hit estimates assume independent legs; shared model errors, book repricing and dependencies remain unmodeled. Each size has a separate record but overlapping tickets are correlated. Goals of 85–90% singles and 65–80% parlays are ambitions, not measured achievements or a professional baseline. Five independent 90% legs yield 59.05%; no success rate is promised.

## Projections and history
Player production rate per minute/opportunity × expected exposure × shrunk opponent positional allowance vs league positional baseline. Position buckets are not individual defender assignments. Football uses attempts/targets, hockey TOI/shots faced, missing exposures flagged. Venue effects require split evidence; teammate-absence adjustments require actual DNP split evidence; rest adjustments are heuristic. Confirmed rotations, defensive assignments, rebound chances, potential assists, schedule strength and full correlation models remain gaps.

Use up to 50 available current-season comparable games from cached schedules (60-day retrieval window), current team when at least five games exist, otherwise current-season data. Outliers stay in the sample; MAD checks and exposure shifts add flags/uncertainty. Early seasons may have fewer games. Charts show actual last 10/15/25 with date/opponent, threshold line and H2H filter; missing games are never fabricated. The 80–90% research screen requires 10+ samples and excludes role/outlier/availability flags; its estimates are not validated hit rates.

Provisional normal/empirical-residual probability ensemble, uncertainty inflation and heuristic risk buffer. Rare events need future count models. Gradual prospective calibration uses only settled forecasts from the same model version and groups correlated alternate lines by event/entity/market/side. Old forecasts are never rewritten. Sport-level calibration is separate from daily selection performance. Legacy research shortlist retained separately from the new official daily ledger.

Free ESPN game/roster/box-score/injury endpoints provide observed DraftKings game-line snapshots where available. No automatic player-prop sportsbook quotes. Suggested lines and illustrative historical-baseline prices (hypothetical 4% overround) are labeled as research estimates, never observed offers. Exact-line editor accepts real prices. Imported personal lines remain on-device and are separate from official performance.

Free Open-Meteo city-level hourly weather is shown when venue resolves without ambiguity. Wind/rain modest heuristic passing/receiving adjustments apply only to confirmed outdoor NFL/CFB venues. Unknown roofs, missing/ambiguous locations and feed errors stay neutral, with gaps labeled. This is not live stadium weather.

## Refresh, persistence and deployment
`.github/workflows/refresh.yml`: every 15 minutes (:07/:22/:37/:52 UTC), source pushes and manual dispatch. GitHub cron may run late. Page polls the latest published feed while visible; Check latest reloads published data, not backend collection. Schedule/roster caches daily; up to 180 boxscores per run, NBA first, remaining queue disclosed. Public free runners and hosting only.

Full immutable line history is preserved in deterministic monthly gzip JSON archives, indexed by `history_manifest.json`. All archive files are published for download; `history.json` holds the latest 5,000 forecasts for fast phone inspection. The entire daily best-pick ledger is published. Shared per-player model details are normalized in a gzip-compressed latest.json.gz and hydrated by the browser to reduce transfer size; current Safari/Chrome support is required. feed_status.json provides a small inspectable build summary. No destructive retention of historical forecasts.

## Validation
`python -m unittest discover -p 'test_*.py'` and `node test_ui.cjs`; syntax checks on app.js, analysis-ui.js and sports-ui.js. Tests cover settlement, calibration, quote sensitivity, immutable daily picks, all ticket sizes, push/pending/loss handling, duplicate-game protection, archives, role/weather checks and charts. Serve with `python -m http.server` for UI checks.

## Permissions
Workflow needs contents:write, pages:write and id-token:write, with GitHub Pages set to Actions. No secrets required. Repository and public forecasts are public. Do not import credentials. No account balances, deposits, sportsbook accounts or wagers are controlled by the app.

## Confidence-first daily policy (v4.1 UI)
Daily v2 ranks positive-value observed quotes by heuristic risk-buffered hit probability, then buffered edge and sample. Singles keep the +/-200 price band and require >=60% model estimate. This is not an 85% success claim. Parlays use >=80% legs at observed -1500 through +200 prices, >=65% independent joint estimate and >=50% buffered product. Quote freshness, 10+ samples, 3–15 pp edge and >=5% expected ROI remain gates. Missing, ambiguous or unresolved player inputs exclude selection. No filler picks. Independence is not verified; the buffered product is not a statistical lower bound. Mathematical joint bounds are shown separately.

Evidence is frozen with each new leg: numerical thesis, scoring or exposure/position inputs, historical threshold chart, quote/break-even/edge, injury and weather feed context, explicit failure cases, source and calibration sample. Team previous-game covers are descriptive against different opponents, not a forecast backtest. Existing old-policy records remain intact and continue grading; new policy is separately identified and daily overview shows current-policy picks. Lifetime scorecards include every policy; a policy update does not reset losses. Every sport has a daily status, including unavailable automatic data, no returned slate and no qualified pick.

The daily alternate-line watchlist is a separate unpriced research surface, up to three stable players from different events per sport. Requires 80–90% model estimate and 80%+ descriptive historical clearing at the exact threshold, ten samples and no unresolved role/availability flags. Rank by the smaller of buffered model probability and historical rate. This deliberately selects on historical performance; it is not an out-of-sample test and no quoted payoff or verified edge is implied. It never enters the official quoted daily ledger. Two/three-leg structures are preferred for research over long tickets; rollover ladder staking is not automated.
