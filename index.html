THE 80–90% SCREEN

Good chance.
Better price.

The 80–90% screen requires 10+ comparable games and rejects role-change, outlier and uncertain-availability flags. It does not remove bad games to improve the hit rate. A high hit estimate can still be a bad price. At -150 you need 60% to break even; at -1500 you need 93.75%. We screen for 3+ pp edge, 5%+ estimated ROI and a positive edge after a model-risk buffer. At least 10 games are required; unusually large claimed edges (>15 pp) are held for market-disagreement review. These are provisional screens, not validated profits.

Ten 90% legs have just a 34.9% combined chance under independence—not 90%. Shared game factors can change that substantially.

Fair odds are a model-implied break-even price, not a sportsbook quote. We do not treat missing prices as value.

Coverage & model health

What goes into the projections?
  • Player models: recent 5 and up to 50 available current-season comparable games (chart shows up to 25); production per minute/opportunity × expected exposure. Opponent allowance for the same role and statistic is compared with the role’s league baseline, shrunk toward neutral. Charts use actual logged games.
  • The same position-rate logic applies to points, rebounds, assists, threes, steals, blocks and observed combos. Football uses attempts/targets when available; hockey uses time-on-ice or shots faced. Missing exposures are flagged.
  • Game models: recent scoring and opponent allowance, with spread, total and team-total projections. Period models use recorded period scores.
  • Injury and teammate absences are flagged. Small venue/absence adjustments require split evidence. Confirmed rotations, individual defensive assignments, potential assists, rebound chances and schedule strength are not fully modeled. Free hourly weather is shown when a venue can be resolved; passing/receiving adjustments require confirmed outdoor conditions.
  • Count/rare-event tails use a provisional normal approximation. Low samples and 90% estimates are not validated betting probabilities.

Coverage, news & model notes
Our selection playbook · why the line and price matter

Alternate-line research: examine thresholds below typical production, stable opportunities and exact-line historical clearing. Prefer a short 2–3-leg core. Longer tickets must still meet the joint-probability screen.

Probability plus price: Linemaker's public setup emphasizes alternate favorites in the -200 to -1000 range, season history and positive price edge. EdgeDeck compares the model with actual quote break-even, then applies a heuristic risk buffer. Historical clearing is supporting evidence, not true next-game probability.

Comparable samples: show current and prior seasons separately when collected, retain outliers, and flag role, minutes, lineup and weather uncertainty. Missing prior-season data stays labeled unavailable. No guaranteed wins or inferred proprietary model.
