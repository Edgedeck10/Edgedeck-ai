THE 90% SCREEN

High hit estimates.
Full context.

A model’s 90% estimate is provisional until prospectively tested. Small samples, changing minutes and injuries can overwhelm the model.

Ten 90% legs have just a 34.9% combined chance under independence—not 90%. Shared game factors can change that substantially.

Fair odds are a model-implied break-even price, not a sportsbook quote. We do not treat missing prices as value.

Prospective calibration

Coverage & model health

What goes into the projections?
  • Player models: recent 5 games and up to 20 available games; expected minutes × per-minute production for basketball; empirical hit rate and streak at each exact threshold.
  • Basketball points: limited opponent team points-allowed adjustment. Rest/back-to-back adjustments. Minutes volatility widens uncertainty.
  • Game models: recent scoring and opponent allowance, with spread, total and team-total projections. Period models use recorded period scores.
  • Injury reports and teammate absences are surfaced. Usage redistribution, confirmed rotations, position-specific defense and schedule strength are not fully modeled.
  • Count/rare-event tails use a provisional normal approximation. Low samples and 90% estimates are not validated betting probabilities.
