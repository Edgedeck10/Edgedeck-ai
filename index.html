Prospective calibration

Alternate lines are correlated. Brier score gives each event / player / market / side group equal weight; raw line counts are not independent trials. Calibration adjusts future estimates gradually, without rewriting old predictions.

Coverage & model health

What goes into the projections?
  • Player models: chronological comparison of available 5–25-game windows; opportunity × production, empirical credibility where estimable, workload scenarios, and conditional predictive distributions.
  • The new player engine does not apply the old arbitrary defensive/rest bonuses. Missing opponent, lineup, weather and usage inputs are shown in each evidence panel.
  • Game models: recent scoring and opponent allowance, with spread, total and team-total projections. Period models use recorded period scores.
  • Injury reports and teammate absences are surfaced. Usage redistribution, confirmed rotations, position-specific defense and schedule strength are not fully modeled.
  • Player props use opportunity-based conditional distributions and approximate uncertainty. Legacy game markets still use a normal approximation. No forecast is validated merely by deployment.
